Weekly Timesheet

Submit every Friday for the full work week (Monday to Friday). Enter time in hours. Smallest unit: 0.5h = 30 min. Standard week: 40 hours (5 days x 8h). If less than 40h, the gap auto-fills Autre (uncategorized). If more than 40h, you'll see a warning but can still submit (overtime is recorded). Resubmit anytime, the latest submission overrides the previous one for the same week.
